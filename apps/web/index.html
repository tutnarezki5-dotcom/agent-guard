Give AI agents money,
not keys

AgentGuard is a policy firewall between an AI agent and its NEAR wallet. Every payment is checked against the task's mandate, the receiver's risk and a vault contract that only pays with a valid signature.

Defense in depth

The agent proposes.
AgentGuard decides.

The model never holds a key and never makes the call. Each payment passes formal rules, a receiver check and on-chain limits, and any one of them can stop it.

Task mandates

Budget, per-payment cap, allowed receivers and expiry belong to one task, not to the whole wallet.

Receiver risk score

Account age, activity, a freshly changed contract and a shared blocklist, scored before every payment.

Two-key payouts

The vault pays only when AgentGuard's Ed25519 signature on the exact terms meets the agent's call.

Instant freeze

One switch stops every payout, in AgentGuard and in the vault contract, at the same moment.

Owner in the loop

Payments above the threshold wait for the owner. Approval re-runs the rules, so it never skips a block.

Replayable decisions

Every verdict can be recomputed from its stored input and lands on the same input hash.
